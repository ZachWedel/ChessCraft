**Colin M.L. Burnett** ([User:Cburnett](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/User:Cburnett)),
which he released under a choice of three licenses: GFDL, BSD and GPL. Chess Craft
uses them under the **BSD license**.

Changes made for Chess Craft: rasterized to 128x128 TGA textures, a soft drop
shadow added, and recolored for the Alliance (blue) and Horde (crimson) set.
